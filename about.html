About ScheduleHub

A premium appointment booking & scheduling platform built for modern businesses

ScheduleHub is a comprehensive demo booking system showcasing modern web development with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. It demonstrates features found in industry-leading platforms like Calendly, Booksy, and Acuity Scheduling.

Built with a futuristic glassmorphism design, the platform includes appointment scheduling, interactive calendars, team management, analytics dashboards, payment UI, video consultations, and extensive demo data.
